What this does and does not do

  • Calls withdraw(uint256), which burns the connected account's own balance.
  • Sends no value with the transaction — the contract sends ETH back to you.
  • Gas comes from the connected account and is estimated by your wallet.
  • Nobody can redeem another address's ABT. If the connected account holds none, this will revert.
